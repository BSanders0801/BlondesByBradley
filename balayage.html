Balayage in Santa Monica

Balayage is a way of placing lightener freehand. The appeal is the soft transition, but the result doesn’t have to be subtle. We can keep the lightness concentrated in a few ribbons or bring more of it through the ends.

When hand-painting is the right choice

I look at your natural base, the color already on your ends and where you want to see the biggest change. If hand-painting alone won’t give us the lift we need, I may combine it with foils or recommend dimensional highlights instead.

If your reference photo has a strong face frame or very pale ends, point out which detail you love. Two photos that both say “balayage” can call for completely different appointments.

Good to know

Is balayage always blonde?

No. It can also bring warmth and dimension to brunette hair while keeping the overall color deep.

How is balayage different from lived-in blonde?

Balayage is a technique. Lived-in blonde describes how the color looks and grows out. A lived-in result can come from hand-painting, foils or both.
