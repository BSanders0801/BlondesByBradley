About Bradley

I’ve spent nearly 30 years as a colorist, salon owner, and educator, with a career that has taken me through New York, Miami, Las Vegas, and now LA. I’ve worked in top salons on Fifth Avenue and in SoHo and spent years in professional education with L’Oréal Professionnel and John Paul Mitchell Systems.

Now based in West Hollywood, I specialize in blondes, brondes, dimensional color, corrections, and transformations, bringing decades of technical experience and a sharp eye for detail to every client.
