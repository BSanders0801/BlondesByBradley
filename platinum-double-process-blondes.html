First-time lightening or a retouch?

A virgin double process is for all-over lightening on untreated hair, followed by glossing to refine the tone. A retouch is for established double-process blonde with new growth. If there’s previous color, banding or uneven regrowth, I need to see it before recommending either appointment.

If you’re considering platinum for the first time or coming from another colorist, reach out before booking. I’d rather give you a realistic plan than promise a specific shade from a photo.

Good to know

Is icy platinum possible in one session?

It depends on your starting level, color history and what your hair can tolerate. Sometimes the right answer is a different shade or more than one visit.

Is a double-process retouch the same as a gloss?

No. The retouch lightens new growth before the tone is refined. A gloss can adjust tone but does not lighten regrowth.
