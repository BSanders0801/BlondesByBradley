Dimensional highlights in Santa Monica

When everything is the same shade of blonde, even beautiful color can look flat. Dimensional highlights keep enough depth between the brighter pieces to make the color move. I place the foils around your part, your face and the way you wear your hair.

Face frame, half or full highlight?

A face frame concentrates brightness around your face. A half highlight adds coverage through the top while leaving more depth underneath. A full highlight carries the color through the back and underneath, which matters if you often wear your hair up or want brightness throughout.

Keeping some of your natural base or adding lowlights can give the brighter pieces more contrast. If you want the softest possible transition instead, see lived-in blonde.

Good to know

Do I need a full highlight every time?

Not always. If you already like the brightness through the back, a half highlight or face frame may be enough for this visit.

Are dimensional highlights only for blondes?

No. On a brunette, the contrast can be much subtler. The goal is for the lighter pieces to show without the whole head reading blonde.
