Before you book

Three things to send me

  • A current photo in indirect daylight
  • A photo of the color you’re after
  • Your recent color and lightening history

What happens at the consultation?

We’ll go over previous lightening, glosses and at-home color, even if they were months ago. I’ll look at the condition of your hair, talk through the result you want and discuss whether a strand test or more than one visit makes sense.

Email a current photo taken in indirect daylight, a reference and whatever you remember about your color history. That gives us a better starting point, but pricing is determined after consultation based on time and product.

Good to know

Is every brassiness issue a color correction?

No. If the lightness is already even and only the tone has shifted, a gloss may be enough. Uneven lightness or bands of different color usually call for a different plan.

Can you tell me how many visits it will take?

I can give you a better estimate after seeing your hair. Previous color, condition and the result you want all matter, so I don’t quote a visit count from a photo alone.
