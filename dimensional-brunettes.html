Dimensional brunette color in Santa Monica

You don’t have to go blonde to see dimension. Sometimes it’s a slightly lighter ribbon around the face. Sometimes it’s adding back the depth that old highlights took away. The aim is to make the brunette more interesting without losing what you like about being brunette.

Decide how much contrast you want

If you like your depth and just want to refine the tone, a gloss may be enough. For richer color all over, single process is another option. If you want to see distinct lighter pieces, we can talk about strategically placed highlights or custom balayage.

Bring a photo of a brunette you like and say what you see in it: the richness, the warmth or just the lighter pieces near the face. That helps me choose a better approach than trying to match a shade name.

Good to know

What’s the difference between brunette and bronde?

A dimensional brunette still reads brown overall. Bronde brings more visible blonde into the mix while keeping a brunette base or deeper pieces.

Can I go darker without making the color look flat?

Yes, depending on your starting point. Keeping some contrast or adding depth selectively can make going darker feel richer without losing the detail.
